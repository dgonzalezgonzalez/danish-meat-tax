# Referee review response, 24 September 2026

This map records the revision against the supplied referee report. It distinguishes completed analysis from claims that the available data cannot support. The manuscript itself remains the authoritative research presentation.

| Referee concern | Revision | Remaining limit |
|---|---|---|
| Grocery history records changes, not daily posted quotes | Reprocessed the frozen source, retained event and snapshot flags, audited within-span event support, rebuilt the diagnostic and price anchor, and removed grocery corroboration claims. See [grocery audit](grocery_history_audit.md). | No historical availability or daily snapshots; monthly posted-price effects cannot be reconstructed from the frozen JSON. |
| Beef classification and package errors | Audited 392 candidate names with Jev, retained 118 strongly supported plain-beef names after explicit false-positive exclusions, excluded mixed livestock controls, and parsed multipacks before unit conversion. Saved decisions work offline. | Name probabilities are not validated ground truth, and package text remains a heuristic. |
| Common European beef shocks and nitrate confound | Promoted the EU27 same-product beef HICP estimate to abstract, main Table 2, results, and discussion; described February, April, June, July, and November policy information. The estimand is the broader cattle-policy episode. | No farm-level variation separately identifies the levy and nitrate change. |
| 2025 timing and uncertain inference | Exported 2024/2025 gap segments and HAC lags 2/3/4/6; discussed the 30 monthly regression observations and the single treated national episode. Country placebo inference provides a distinct, assumption-dependent comparison. | Neither placebo repetitions nor product/pair clustering create additional Danish policy episodes; no strong tax-specific significance claim is made. |
| Aggregate event-study regressors and intervals | Removed identically zero adjustment interactions and saturated-regression pointwise bars; the official plot now shows the descriptive monthly gap. The grocery plot uses genuine calendar event spacing with June empty. | Grocery event bars describe product–store conditional variation only. |
| Window choice and synthetic donor validation | Removed the all-outcome veto, put 15/24/36/54 point estimates and common-period fit into Appendix Table B3, separated long-window seasonality, exported country unit/time weights, donor concentration, donor-drop and short holdout gaps. | Holdouts are short; the April–June 2024 block already contains policy news. In-sample fit does not validate the post-period counterfactual. |
| Theory magnitude and livestock margins | Added a finite two-date price bound, numerical comparison with the announced average burden, herd stock-flow and carcass-weight accounting, trade and retail margins, and average-burden versus marginal-abatement distinction. | The model cannot explain the retail contrast quantitatively without unobserved credibility, taxable intensity, elasticities, multiple dates, and pass-through. |
| Slaughter and trade mechanisms | Estimated mean carcass weight directly on common source cells; retained all production sensitivities; limited the extra-EU pair estimate to its actual `log(1+tonnes)` estimand. | Herd size, live-animal and intra-EU trade, total national imports, and origin-specific consumer prices are unavailable in the current research inputs. |
| SCC interpretation and bases | Reframed as hypothetical DKK/kg accounting, introduced separate causal attribution `q` and persistence `a`, added a zero-attribution scenario, and updated all figures from the screened provisional level anchor. Distinct lifecycle-damage, average output-burden, and marginal incentive objects are explicit. | Selected SCC studies do not define a common 2030 sampling distribution; the level anchor is not a validated posted-price index. No welfare or optimal-tax conclusion is drawn. |
| Provenance and presentation | Kept the input hash manifest unchanged, recorded Jev decisions without the API key, added automated TeX-to-CSV number checks, updated output documentation, fixed Table 2 observation counts, chronology, figure notes, and revision date. | Public historical snapshot access, redistribution rights, an immutable archive, and clean-machine execution remain unresolved and are not claimed. |

The revised contribution is a descriptive, theoretically disciplined analysis of a cattle-policy transition. A tax-specific causal estimate would require new data on nitrate exposure and expected emissions liabilities or another credible source of independent variation.
