# Response to the third-round referee report

The third-round review assessed commit `44007e9` as a bounded revise-and-resubmit for the paper's stated descriptive contribution. It independently reconstructed the official country timing, donor and time-weight concentration, domestic DiD and HAC sensitivity, grocery aggregate reconciliation, model-magnitude arithmetic, and the sensitivity-table entries. It did not execute the full Stata workflow or inspect the committed PDF visually. The changes below address its finite conditions while retaining the distinction between a descriptive comparison and an isolated tax effect.

## Empirical interpretation and measurement

The abstract, discussion, and conclusion now distinguish the smaller country point contrast from a statistical upper bound. The country interval admits economically relevant Danish-specific price movements and the comparison does not assign a share of the domestic change to European shocks. The discussion states the general economic lesson: a domestic broad-food comparison can mix a local policy episode with a product-specific international movement, so the choice of comparator changes the descriptive object. The manuscript's grocery-data paragraph now says that a text-only assistant review inspected retained product descriptions. It does not imply independent human adjudication or validated classification precision, package contents, or historical availability. The Results section now correctly describes the dependent variable as the mean of price **levels recorded at change events**.

## Appendix, transcription, and display

The former Appendix Table B3 domestic CPI 36-month coefficient was `0.062`, although its Stata output is `0.06146`; direct three-decimal rounding is `0.061`. The table now reports `0.061`. It also gives the country-HICP RMSE ratios to the 15-month baseline (`1.000`, `1.023`, `0.960`, `0.971`), exposing the small fit improvements hidden when every RMSE prints as `0.010`. `scripts/verify_paper_numbers.py` now checks each of the table's 20 ATT and 20 RMSE cells against keyed Stata rows, the HICP ratio row, Table 2 significance stars, and Table 3 estimator headings. Focused tests require failure for the previously wrong appendix cell and a swapped Table 3 header. The SCC inventory has moved from Appendix B to Appendix C, where it is Table C1; production and window-sensitivity tables are now B1 and B2. Cross-references are generated from labels.

## Evidence preservation

The six exact official source files needed for the national CPI, country HICP, and slaughter analyses are preserved as the `official_inputs_20260930.zip` asset of the versioned `official-inputs-2026-09-30` GitHub release. Its committed access path and ZIP checksum appear in `data/reference/frozen_official/archive_manifest.json`; individual source bytes match the **unchanged** research-input SHA-256 manifest. This external deposit respects the repository instruction to exclude raw-data commits. Statistics Denmark's StatBank API gives CC BY 4.0 reuse terms, and Eurostat authorizes reuse of its statistics with attribution; the archive README cites both providers and the root README identifies source queries and provenance. The official-only target retrieves missing cached sources, verifies the ZIP and each source before restoring missing working copies, leaves existing files untouched, then verifies the working inputs before preprocessing and Stata estimation. This supplies a fresh clone with the central official evidence, provided the documented Stata/MP 19.5 and `sdid` 2.0.2 environment is available. The commercial Stata runtime and third-party `sdid` package are not bundled. The versioned access path is not a claim of a perpetual preservation guarantee.

The complete 45,637-unit grocery eligibility reconciliation ledger is committed as `data/reference/panel_eligibility_reconciliation.csv`, SHA-256 `9aa51607beb45c38c99efaf69622c518489b3d53ce28cded9ce9f4fcce2728c2`. ## Remaining scope

The official coefficients retain their reported uncertainty and are not re-estimated by these textual and preservation changes. The grocery screen remains assistant-assisted rather than independently human validated. The official source archive and eligibility ledger improve reproducibility; they do not make the optional grocery, trade, and SCC stages exactly reproducible from a fresh clone. The country and domestic indices use different comparators, and their point-estimate difference is not a formal decomposition of European and Danish shocks.
